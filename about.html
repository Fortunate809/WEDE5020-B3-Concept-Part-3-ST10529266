Who We Are

B3 Concept was founded in 2015 as a small startup providing interior design services to residential clients. The organisation has since expanded its service offering and works with residential and commercial clients while developing its presence in other parts of South Africa.

Mission

To build enduring interiors through aligned goals of innovation, creativity and excellence.

Our Approach

B3 Concept explains that successful design needs a plan, a strategy and a story. Each project is assessed individually so the final solution can respond to the client's requirements, scope and budget.

Why Clients Choose B3 Concept

  • Professional interior design solutions
  • Client-centred project planning
  • Experience across residential and corporate environments
  • Tailor-made solutions
  • Network of suppliers, contractors and architects
